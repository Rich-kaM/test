# Pre-launch verification checklist

- [ ] Confirm exact legal name and footer wording.
- [ ] Confirm official phone number.
- [ ] Confirm official email.
- [ ] Confirm physical address.
- [ ] Confirm domain.
- [ ] Confirm registration and tax details.
- [ ] Confirm privacy contact and legal representative.
- [ ] Confirm expert titles, biographies and translations.
- [ ] Record image ownership and written consent.
- [ ] Add approved social accounts.
- [ ] Review bilingual legal pages.
- [ ] Select and configure CAPTCHA.
- [ ] Configure production email provider in sandbox/test mode first.
- [ ] Configure newsletter double opt-in.
- [ ] Define recruitment retention period.
- [ ] Complete security, accessibility, performance and SEO audits.
